Good to know

Which bonus will I get?

The bonus for your VIP level at the moment your friend's successful trades reach a total of 100 points: from ₦2,000 (Bronze) up to ₦6,000 (Diamond).

When does an invite qualify?

When your friend has joined with your code and their successful trades between 25 Nov and 31 Dec 2026 add up to at least 100 points. It can be one order or several.

Is my commission doubled too?

No. The one-time Direct Invite Bonus is doubled; your commission on your network's trades stays the same.

How is the bonus paid?

Automatically to your Tbay wallet once the invite qualifies. You don't need to claim it.

Is there a limit on invites?

No. There's no limit: you earn the doubled bonus for every friend who qualifies.

  • The bonus follows your VIP level when the invite qualifies. Commission rates are unchanged.
  • There is no limit on the number of qualifying invites. Normal referral rules apply.
